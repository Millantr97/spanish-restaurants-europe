Spanish food in Vilnius: a guide by occasion

Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Based on 4 tracked Spanish venues in Vilnius · Best-ranked list

Not every Spanish restaurant fits every plan. Using the Google ratings, review counts and the dishes we have confirmed on each menu, here is how we would play Vilnius depending on the occasion.

For a date

Pulpo (Stiklių g. 16, 01131 Vilnius, Lithuania) — the strongest all-round rating in the city (★ 4.3 from 423 reviews; the menu confirms patatas bravas, croquetas, Galician-style octopus (pulpo a la gallega), Padrón peppers and churros). Book ahead at weekends.

A special occasion

California Tapas & Wine Bar (Subačiaus g. 2, 01127 Vilnius, Lithuania) — ★ 4.1 from 587 reviews; the menu confirms chorizo and ensaladilla rusa. Tell them you are celebrating when you book; Spanish restaurants are usually generous with a celebration table.

A quick, casual bite

Nepatinka NEIK - Spanish PUB (J. Basanavičiaus g. 2, 01118 Vilnius, Lithuania) — ★ 4.8 from 445 reviews; around €5-€20 per person. Bar seating and a couple of tapas is the fastest way to eat well here.
