Spanish food in Prague: a guide by occasion

Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Based on 6 tracked Spanish venues in Prague · Best-ranked list

Not every Spanish restaurant fits every plan. Using the Google ratings, review counts and the dishes we have confirmed on each menu, here is how we would play Prague depending on the occasion.

For a date

El Camino Tapas Restaurant (Jagellonská 10, 130 00 Praha 3-Vinohrady, Czechia) — the strongest all-round rating in the city (★ 4.9 from 1,043 reviews; the menu confirms torrijas). Book ahead at weekends.

Tapas with friends

La Boca (Truhlářská 10, 110 00 Praha 1-Petrská čtvrť, Czechia) — the widest confirmed Spanish spread in Prague: croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, paella, Padrón peppers and chorizo (★ 4.4 from 1,994 reviews; the menu confirms croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, paella, Padrón peppers and chorizo). Order several plates to share and ask what is on special.

A special occasion

Tapas Bar Miró (Štefánikova 46, 150 00 Praha 5-Smíchov, Czechia) — ★ 4.5 from 942 reviews; the menu confirms patatas bravas, croquetas, fried calamari and albóndigas (meatballs). Tell them you are celebrating when you book; Spanish restaurants are usually generous with a celebration table.

A quick, casual bite

Jamonarna (Bělohorská 43, 169 00 Praha 6, Czechia) — ★ 4.9 from 132 reviews; the menu confirms Ibérico/serrano ham, paella and chorizo. Bar seating and a couple of tapas is the fastest way to eat well here.
