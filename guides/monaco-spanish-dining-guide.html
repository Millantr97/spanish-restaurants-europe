Spanish food in Monaco: a guide by occasion

Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Based on 3 tracked Spanish venues in Monaco · Best-ranked list

Not every Spanish restaurant fits every plan. Using the Google ratings, review counts and the dishes we have confirmed on each menu, here is how we would play Monaco depending on the occasion.

For a date

Le corner by 3 tapas (Rue du Marché, 06240 Beausoleil, France) — the strongest all-round rating in the city (★ 4.8 from 70 reviews). Book ahead at weekends.

Tapas with friends

Restaurant l'Ecureuil Espagnol (17 rue Fourmilières, 06600 Antibes, France) — the widest confirmed Spanish spread in Monaco: Ibérico/serrano ham, paella and chorizo (★ 4.5 from 340 reviews; the menu confirms Ibérico/serrano ham, paella and chorizo; around €9-€22 per person). Order several plates to share and ask what is on special.

A special occasion

A quick, casual bite
