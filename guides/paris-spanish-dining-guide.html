Spanish food in Paris: a guide by occasion

Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Based on 50 tracked Spanish venues in Paris · Best-ranked list

Not every Spanish restaurant fits every plan. Using the Google ratings, review counts and the dishes we have confirmed on each menu, here is how we would play Paris depending on the occasion.

For a date

Les Piétons (8 Rue des Lombards, 75004 Paris, France) — the strongest all-round rating in the city (★ 4.3 from 5,170 reviews; the menu confirms patatas bravas, croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, Galician-style octopus (pulpo a la gallega), fried calamari, pan con tomate, paella, Padrón peppers, albóndigas (meatballs), chorizo, ensaladilla rusa, churros and gazpacho / salmorejo). Book ahead at weekends.

Order several plates to share and ask what is on special.

A special occasion

Caves Saint Gilles (4 Rue Saint-Gilles, 75003 Paris, France) — ★ 4.2 from 1,893 reviews; the menu confirms patatas bravas, croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, Galician-style octopus (pulpo a la gallega), fried calamari, pan con tomate, paella and ensaladilla rusa. Tell them you are celebrating when you book; Spanish restaurants are usually generous with a celebration table.

A quick, casual bite

Bodega Potxolo (7 Rue de la Forge Royale, 75011 Paris, France) — ★ 4.9 from 596 reviews; the menu confirms fried calamari, albóndigas (meatballs) and chorizo; around €30-€40 per person. Bar seating and a couple of tapas is the fastest way to eat well here.

Something sweet to finish

Les Piétons (8 Rue des Lombards, 75004 Paris, France) — one of the few places in Paris whose menu confirms churros.
