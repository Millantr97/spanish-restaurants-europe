Spanish food in Paris: a guide by occasion

Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Based on 132 tracked Spanish venues in Paris · Best-ranked list

Not every Spanish restaurant fits every plan. Using the Google ratings, review counts and the dishes we have confirmed on each menu, here is how we would play Paris depending on the occasion.

For a date

La Abuela (10 Bd de Clichy, 75018 Paris, France) — the strongest all-round rating in the city (★ 4.5 from 2,131 reviews). Book ahead at weekends.

Tapas with friends

Les Piétons (8 Rue des Lombards, 75004 Paris, France) — the widest confirmed Spanish spread in Paris: patatas bravas, Ibérico/serrano ham, Galician-style octopus (pulpo a la gallega), fried calamari, pan con tomate, paella, albóndigas (meatballs), chorizo, ensaladilla rusa, churros and gazpacho / salmorejo (★ 4.3 from 5,170 reviews; the menu confirms patatas bravas, Ibérico/serrano ham, Galician-style octopus (pulpo a la gallega), fried calamari, pan con tomate, paella, albóndigas (meatballs), chorizo, ensaladilla rusa, churros and gazpacho / salmorejo). Order several plates to share and ask what is on special.

A special occasion

Bodega Potxolo (7 Rue de la Forge Royale, 75011 Paris, France) — ★ 4.9 from 596 reviews; the menu confirms fried calamari, albóndigas (meatballs) and chorizo; around €30-€40 per person. Tell them you are celebrating when you book; Spanish restaurants are usually generous with a celebration table.

A quick, casual bite

Le P'tit Barcelone (10 Rue de la Boule Rouge, 75009 Paris, France) — ★ 4.3 from 1,283 reviews; the menu confirms patatas bravas, croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, garlic prawns (gambas al ajillo), fried calamari, pan con tomate and chorizo. Bar seating and a couple of tapas is the fastest way to eat well here.
