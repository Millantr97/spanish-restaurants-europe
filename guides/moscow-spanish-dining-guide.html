Spanish food in Moscow: a guide by occasion

Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Based on 3 tracked Spanish venues in Moscow · Best-ranked list

Not every Spanish restaurant fits every plan. Using the Google ratings, review counts and the dishes we have confirmed on each menu, here is how we would play Moscow depending on the occasion.

For a date

Па-Паэлья Pa-Paella (Bankovskiy Pereulok, 5, Moskva, Russia, 101000) — the strongest all-round rating in the city (★ 4.5 from 1,019 reviews). Book ahead at weekends.

Tapas with friends

El Asador (Ulitsa Bol'shaya Ordynka, 45/8, Moskva, Russia, 115184) — the widest confirmed Spanish spread in Moscow: chorizo (★ 4.4 from 927 reviews; the menu confirms chorizo). Order several plates to share and ask what is on special.

A special occasion

Ispanskaya Lavka (Myasnitskaya Ulitsa, 30/3, Moskva, Russia, 125075) — ★ 4.5 from 443 reviews. Tell them you are celebrating when you book; Spanish restaurants are usually generous with a celebration table.

A quick, casual bite

Tapas & Avocado Bar Moscow (Nizhnyaya Krasnosel'skaya Ulitsa, 35, стр.59, Moskva, Russia, 105066) — ★ 4.0 from 6 reviews. Bar seating and a couple of tapas is the fastest way to eat well here.

Something sweet to finish

WE Cidreria (Lubyanskiy Proyezd, 15с2, Moskva, Russia, 101000) — one of the few places in Moscow whose menu confirms churros.
