Spanish food in Copenhagen: a guide by occasion

Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Based on 8 tracked Spanish venues in Copenhagen · Best-ranked list

Not every Spanish restaurant fits every plan. Using the Google ratings, review counts and the dishes we have confirmed on each menu, here is how we would play Copenhagen depending on the occasion.

For a date

Pincho Nation (Fiolstræde 44, 1171 København, Denmark) — the strongest all-round rating in the city (★ 4.2 from 1,731 reviews). Book ahead at weekends.

Tapas with friends

El Meson (Hauser Pl. 12, 1127 København, Denmark) — the widest confirmed Spanish spread in Copenhagen: patatas bravas, croquetas, garlic prawns (gambas al ajillo), pan con tomate, paella, albóndigas (meatballs) and chorizo (★ 4.2 from 737 reviews; the menu confirms patatas bravas, croquetas, garlic prawns (gambas al ajillo), pan con tomate, paella, albóndigas (meatballs) and chorizo). Order several plates to share and ask what is on special.

A special occasion

Fest og Tapas (Gammel Køge Landevej 264A, 2650 Hvidovre, Denmark) — ★ 4.6 from 111 reviews; the menu confirms croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, garlic prawns (gambas al ajillo), albóndigas (meatballs) and chorizo. Tell them you are celebrating when you book; Spanish restaurants are usually generous with a celebration table.

A quick, casual bite

Temporada Tapas og Vin (Åboulevard 23, st tv, 1960 Frederiksberg, Denmark) — ★ 4.7 from 752 reviews; the menu confirms patatas bravas, croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham and chorizo. Bar seating and a couple of tapas is the fastest way to eat well here.
