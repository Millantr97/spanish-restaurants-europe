Spanish food in Podgorica: a guide by occasion

Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Based on 2 tracked Spanish venues in Podgorica · Best-ranked list

Not every Spanish restaurant fits every plan. Using the Google ratings, review counts and the dishes we have confirmed on each menu, here is how we would play Podgorica depending on the occasion.

For a date

TAPATEO tapas bar (43 Studentska, Podgorica, Montenegro) — the strongest all-round rating in the city (★ 4.7 from 60 reviews). Book ahead at weekends.

Tapas with friends

Mañana Cocktail Bar and Food (Cara Dusana, 5 Trg Palmi, Budva 85310, Montenegro) — a solid all-round tapas option (★ 4.6 from 558 reviews; around €5-€15 per person). Order several plates to share and ask what is on special.

A special occasion

Mañana Cocktail Bar and Food (Cara Dusana, 5 Trg Palmi, Budva 85310, Montenegro) — ★ 4.6 from 558 reviews; around €5-€15 per person. Tell them you are celebrating when you book; Spanish restaurants are usually generous with a celebration table.
