Spanish food in Luxembourg: a guide by occasion

Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Based on 8 tracked Spanish venues in Luxembourg · Best-ranked list

Not every Spanish restaurant fits every plan. Using the Google ratings, review counts and the dishes we have confirmed on each menu, here is how we would play Luxembourg depending on the occasion.

For a date

PODENCO Bodega (1 Rue de l'Industrie, 8069 Bertrange, Luxembourg) — the strongest all-round rating in the city (★ 4.5 from 330 reviews; the menu confirms croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, paella and chorizo; around €30-€70 per person). Book ahead at weekends.

Tapas with friends

PODENCO Bodega (1 Rue de l'Industrie, 8069 Bertrange, Luxembourg) — the widest confirmed Spanish spread in Luxembourg: croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, paella and chorizo (★ 4.5 from 330 reviews; the menu confirms croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, paella and chorizo; around €30-€70 per person). A special occasion

El Barrio Restaurante y Tapas Bar (2 Rue Erasme, 1468 Kirchberg Luxembourg) — ★ 4.4 from 3,831 reviews. Tell them you are celebrating when you book; Spanish restaurants are usually generous with a celebration table.

A quick, casual bite

La Tapería (120 Rue Albert Unden, 2652 Limpertsberg Luxembourg) — ★ 4.4 from 700 reviews. Bar seating and a couple of tapas is the fastest way to eat well here.
