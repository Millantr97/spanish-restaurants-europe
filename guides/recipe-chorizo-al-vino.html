Chorizo al vino (chorizo braised in wine)

⏱ 25 min · Very easy · 4 as a tapa · All recipes

Chorizo is usually eaten sliced as-is, but gently braised in red wine (or cider, in Asturias) it turns into one of the great bar tapas: the paprika fat melts into a sauce that demands bread.

Ingredients

  • 400 g cooking chorizo (semi-cured; ask for chorizo para cocinar), cut into 1 cm slices
  • 250 ml red wine (or dry cider for chorizo a la sidra)
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2 garlic cloves, smashed
  • Bread to serve

Method

  1. Put everything in a small pan - no oil needed, the chorizo brings its own fat.
  2. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered for 15-20 minutes, until the wine reduces to a glossy, paprika-red sauce.
  3. Serve hot in a cazuela with the sauce and plenty of bread.

Tips

  • Use cooking chorizo, not the fully cured slicing kind: it stays juicy.
  • Leftovers make an outrageous sandwich the next day.
