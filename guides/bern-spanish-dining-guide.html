Spanish food in Bern: a guide by occasion

Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Based on 13 tracked Spanish venues in Bern · Best-ranked list

Not every Spanish restaurant fits every plan. Using the Google ratings, review counts and the dishes we have confirmed on each menu, here is how we would play Bern depending on the occasion.

For a date

Volver BarTapasCafé (Rathauspl. 8, 3011 Bern, Switzerland) — the strongest all-round rating in the city (★ 4.5 from 942 reviews; the menu confirms fried calamari and chorizo). Book ahead at weekends.

Tapas with friends

Restaurant Au Rendez-Vous Biel/Bienne Espagnol Español Spanisch Tapas Bar (Mühlebrücke 8, 2502 Biel, Switzerland) — the widest confirmed Spanish spread in Bern: Ibérico/serrano ham, pan con tomate and paella (★ 4.8 from 228 reviews; the menu confirms Ibérico/serrano ham, pan con tomate and paella). Order several plates to share and ask what is on special.

A special occasion

La Taberna (Rue Centrale 43, 1580 Avenches, Switzerland) — ★ 4.6 from 119 reviews. Tell them you are celebrating when you book; Spanish restaurants are usually generous with a celebration table.

A quick, casual bite

Bar De Vinos (Herzogstrasse 12, 3014 Bern, Switzerland) — ★ 5.0 from 21 reviews. Bar seating and a couple of tapas is the fastest way to eat well here.
