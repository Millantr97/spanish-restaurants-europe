Spanish food in Budapest: a guide by occasion

Updated 10 Sep 2026 · Based on 16 tracked Spanish venues in Budapest · Best-ranked list

Not every Spanish restaurant fits every plan. Using the Google ratings, review counts and the dishes we have confirmed on each menu, here is how we would play Budapest depending on the occasion.

For a date

Tapas Bar by Felisa (Budapest, Zrínyi u. 16, 1051 Hungary) — the strongest all-round rating in the city (★ 4.6 from 6,193 reviews; the menu confirms fried calamari, Padrón peppers and chorizo). Book ahead at weekends.

Tapas with friends

Saboré - Gluten Free Tapas Restaurant (Budapest, Falk Miksa u. 21, 1055 Hungary) — the widest confirmed Spanish spread in Budapest: patatas bravas, croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, garlic prawns (gambas al ajillo), Galician-style octopus (pulpo a la gallega), fried calamari, Padrón peppers, chorizo and gazpacho / salmorejo (★ 4.7 from 512 reviews; the menu confirms patatas bravas, croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, garlic prawns (gambas al ajillo), Galician-style octopus (pulpo a la gallega), fried calamari, Padrón peppers, chorizo and gazpacho / salmorejo). Order several plates to share and ask what is on special.

A special occasion

Tapassio (Budapest, Hercegprímás u. 13, 1051 Hungary) — ★ 4.8 from 2,926 reviews; the menu confirms patatas bravas, croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, garlic prawns (gambas al ajillo), Galician-style octopus (pulpo a la gallega), fried calamari and chorizo. Tell them you are celebrating when you book; Spanish restaurants are usually generous with a celebration table.

A quick, casual bite

La Nube Tapas & Wine Restaurant (Budapest, Bartók Béla út 41, 1114 Hungary) — ★ 4.7 from 1,852 reviews; the menu confirms croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham and churros. Bar seating and a couple of tapas is the fastest way to eat well here.

Something sweet to finish

La Nube Tapas & Wine Restaurant (Budapest, Bartók Béla út 41, 1114 Hungary) — one of the few places in Budapest whose menu confirms churros.
