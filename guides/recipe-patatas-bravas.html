Patatas bravas

⏱ 40 min · Easy · 4 as a tapa · All recipes

Crispy fried potatoes with a spicy tomato-based salsa brava and, in much of Spain, a blob of alioli. Every bar guards its own brava sauce; this version is the classic Madrid style, built on pimentón.

Ingredients

  • 4 large floury potatoes, peeled and cut into 3 cm chunks
  • Olive oil for frying
  • 1 tsp sweet pimentón (smoked if possible)
  • 1/2 tsp hot pimentón or a pinch of cayenne
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 250 ml chicken or vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp sherry vinegar (or white wine vinegar)
  • Salt
  • Alioli to serve (optional)

Method

  1. Parboil the potatoes in salted water for 8 minutes, drain and let them steam dry.
  2. For the salsa brava: heat 3 tablespoons of oil, stir in both pimentóns off the heat so they do not burn, then the flour. Cook 1 minute and whisk in the stock little by little. Simmer 8-10 minutes until thick, add the vinegar and salt, and blend until smooth.
  3. Fry the potatoes in plenty of hot olive oil (180 C) until deep golden and crisp, about 8-10 minutes. Drain on paper and salt generously.
  4. Serve with the hot brava sauce poured over and alioli on top.

Tips

  • Parboiling first is what gives the fluffy inside and glassy crust.
  • The sauce should be thick enough to cling to the potato, never runny.
