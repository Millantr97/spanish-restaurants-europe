Spanish food in Ljubljana: a guide by occasion

Updated 10 Sep 2026 · Based on 1 tracked Spanish venue in Ljubljana · Best-ranked list

Not every Spanish restaurant fits every plan. Using the Google ratings, review counts and the dishes we have confirmed on each menu, here is how we would play Ljubljana depending on the occasion.

For a date

La Santa Maria Tapas Bar (Slovenska cesta 16, 1000 Ljubljana, Slovenia) — the strongest all-round rating in the city (★ 3.8 from 28 reviews; around €15-€20 per person). Book ahead at weekends.

Tapas with friends

La Santa Maria Tapas Bar (Slovenska cesta 16, 1000 Ljubljana, Slovenia) — a solid all-round tapas option (★ 3.8 from 28 reviews; around €15-€20 per person). Order several plates to share and ask what is on special.
