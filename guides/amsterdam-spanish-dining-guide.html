Spanish food in Amsterdam: a guide by occasion

Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Based on 17 tracked Spanish venues in Amsterdam · Best-ranked list

Not every Spanish restaurant fits every plan. Using the Google ratings, review counts and the dishes we have confirmed on each menu, here is how we would play Amsterdam depending on the occasion.

For a date

El Pimpi Tapas (Stromarkt 4, 1012 SW Amsterdam, Netherlands) — the strongest all-round rating in the city (★ 4.7 from 2,154 reviews; the menu confirms Spanish omelette (tortilla española), patatas bravas, croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, garlic prawns (gambas al ajillo), fried calamari, pan con tomate, paella, albóndigas (meatballs) and chorizo). Book ahead at weekends.

Tapas with friends

Pata Negra (Utrechtsestraat 124, 1017 VT Amsterdam, Netherlands) — the widest confirmed Spanish spread in Amsterdam: patatas bravas, croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, garlic prawns (gambas al ajillo), Galician-style octopus (pulpo a la gallega), fried calamari, Padrón peppers, albóndigas (meatballs), chorizo, ensaladilla rusa and gazpacho / salmorejo (★ 4.2 from 2,024 reviews; the menu confirms patatas bravas, croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, garlic prawns (gambas al ajillo), Galician-style octopus (pulpo a la gallega), fried calamari, Padrón peppers, albóndigas (meatballs), chorizo, ensaladilla rusa and gazpacho / salmorejo). Order several plates to share and ask what is on special.

A special occasion

SOL EL LUNA (Ferdinand Bolstraat 158, 1072 LT Amsterdam, Netherlands) — ★ 4.5 from 630 reviews; the menu confirms Spanish omelette (tortilla española), patatas bravas, croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, garlic prawns (gambas al ajillo), Galician-style octopus (pulpo a la gallega), pan con tomate, paella, ensaladilla rusa and gazpacho / salmorejo. Tell them you are celebrating when you book; Spanish restaurants are usually generous with a celebration table.

A quick, casual bite

Mamas & Tapas (Hugo de Grootplein 6, 1052 KW Amsterdam, Netherlands) — ★ 4.8 from 1,404 reviews; the menu confirms patatas bravas and croquetas; around €20-€40 per person. Bar seating and a couple of tapas is the fastest way to eat well here.

Something sweet to finish

Café Josélito (Nieuwendijk 2, 1012 MK Amsterdam, Netherlands) — one of the few places in Amsterdam whose menu confirms churros.
