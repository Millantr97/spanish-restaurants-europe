Spanish food in Oslo: a guide by occasion

Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Based on 7 tracked Spanish venues in Oslo · Best-ranked list

Not every Spanish restaurant fits every plan. Using the Google ratings, review counts and the dishes we have confirmed on each menu, here is how we would play Oslo depending on the occasion.

For a date

La Sangria Tapas Restaurant (Holbergs gate 19, 0166 Oslo, Norway) — the strongest all-round rating in the city (★ 4.4 from 666 reviews; the menu confirms patatas bravas, Ibérico/serrano ham, garlic prawns (gambas al ajillo), pan con tomate, paella, Padrón peppers, albóndigas (meatballs), chorizo and gazpacho / salmorejo). Book ahead at weekends.

A special occasion

Barramón Mathallen (Maridalsveien 17, 0178 Oslo, Norway) — ★ 4.7 from 169 reviews; the menu confirms patatas bravas, Ibérico/serrano ham, Padrón peppers and chorizo. Tell them you are celebrating when you book; Spanish restaurants are usually generous with a celebration table.

A quick, casual bite

Serrano's Bodega (Strømsveien 245, 0668 Oslo, Norway) — ★ 4.5 from 82 reviews; the menu confirms Ibérico/serrano ham and paella. Bar seating and a couple of tapas is the fastest way to eat well here.
