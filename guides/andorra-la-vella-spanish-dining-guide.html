Spanish food in Andorra la Vella: a guide by occasion

Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Based on 4 tracked Spanish venues in Andorra la Vella · Best-ranked list

Not every Spanish restaurant fits every plan. Using the Google ratings, review counts and the dishes we have confirmed on each menu, here is how we would play Andorra la Vella depending on the occasion.

For a date

Alta Taberna Aires Gourmet (Carrer Bonaventura Riberaygua, 9, AD500 Andorra la Vella, Andorra) — the strongest all-round rating in the city (★ 4.9 from 686 reviews). Book ahead at weekends.

Tapas with friends

Alta Taberna Aires Gourmet (Carrer Bonaventura Riberaygua, 9, AD500 Andorra la Vella, Andorra) — a solid all-round tapas option (★ 4.9 from 686 reviews). Order several plates to share and ask what is on special.

A special occasion

LIZARRAN TABERNA ANDORRA (Av. Meritxell, 86, AD500 Andorra la Vella, Andorra) — ★ 3.1 from 1,224 reviews. Tell them you are celebrating when you book; Spanish restaurants are usually generous with a celebration table.

A quick, casual bite

Taberna del Mayor (Carrer Verge del Pilar, 6, AD500 Andorra la Vella, Andorra) — ★ 4.7 from 103 reviews. Bar seating and a couple of tapas is the fastest way to eat well here.
