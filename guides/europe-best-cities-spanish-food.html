The best cities in Europe for Spanish food, ranked

Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Based on 8,355 tracked Spanish restaurants and tapas bars across Europe

We ranked every European city in our database by the number of Spanish restaurants and tapas bars we have verified there, with the average Google rating and total review count as evidence of depth. Spain itself is excluded: this map covers the diaspora. Counts reflect cities we have searched so far, not a census.

The per 100k column normalizes each city's venue count by its city-proper population - it is the density of Spanish tables per 100,000 inhabitants. Populations come from national statistics offices (compiled in Wikipedia's city population lists), checked 11 Sep 2026. Boundary note: London is counted as Greater London and Brussels as the Brussels-Capital Region, matching how our map pins cover the urban area; for the rest, city-proper boundaries apply, so where a statistical boundary is narrower than the area our listings cover, the rate is an approximation and we say so here rather than hide it.
