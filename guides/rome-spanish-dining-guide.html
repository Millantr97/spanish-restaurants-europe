Spanish food in Rome: a guide by occasion

Updated 10 Sep 2026 · Based on 5 tracked Spanish venues in Rome · Best-ranked list

Not every Spanish restaurant fits every plan. Using the Google ratings, review counts and the dishes we have confirmed on each menu, here is how we would play Rome depending on the occasion.

For a date

Pequeño | Atypical Tapas Bar (Largo Somalia, 39, 00199 Roma RM, Italy) — the strongest all-round rating in the city (★ 4.8 from 774 reviews). Book ahead at weekends.

Tapas with friends

llama y tapas - Cucina Spagnola (Viale di Valle Aurelia, 30, 00167 Roma RM, Italy) — the widest confirmed Spanish spread in Rome: Spanish omelette (tortilla española), patatas bravas, croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, Galician-style octopus (pulpo a la gallega), pan con tomate, paella, chorizo, gazpacho / salmorejo and torrijas (★ 4.3 from 7 reviews; the menu confirms Spanish omelette (tortilla española), patatas bravas, croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, Galician-style octopus (pulpo a la gallega), pan con tomate, paella, chorizo, gazpacho / salmorejo and torrijas). Order several plates to share and ask what is on special.

A special occasion

La Casa Iberica Roma (Viale Parioli, 144, 00197 Roma RM, Italy) — ★ 3.8 from 105 reviews; the menu confirms Ibérico/serrano ham and paella; around €30-€40 per person. Tell them you are celebrating when you book; Spanish restaurants are usually generous with a celebration table.

A quick, casual bite

chiringuito bistrot (Via di S. Francesco a Ripa, 165, 00153 Roma RM, Italy) — ★ 4.7 from 309 reviews. Bar seating and a couple of tapas is the fastest way to eat well here.
