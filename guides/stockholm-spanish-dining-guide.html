Spanish food in Stockholm: a guide by occasion

Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Based on 29 tracked Spanish venues in Stockholm · Best-ranked list

Not every Spanish restaurant fits every plan. Using the Google ratings, review counts and the dishes we have confirmed on each menu, here is how we would play Stockholm depending on the occasion.

For a date

El Cielo - Tapas & Cava (Fleminggatan 22a, 112 32 Stockholm, Sweden) — the strongest all-round rating in the city (★ 4.4 from 2,484 reviews; the menu confirms patatas bravas, Ibérico/serrano ham, fried calamari, Padrón peppers, chorizo and churros). Book ahead at weekends.

Tapas with friends

Ramblas (Sankt Paulsgatan 22D, 118 48 Stockholm, Sweden) — the widest confirmed Spanish spread in Stockholm: Spanish omelette (tortilla española), patatas bravas, croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, garlic prawns (gambas al ajillo), fried calamari, pan con tomate, paella, Padrón peppers, chorizo and churros (★ 4.1 from 1,753 reviews; the menu confirms Spanish omelette (tortilla española), patatas bravas, croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, garlic prawns (gambas al ajillo), fried calamari, pan con tomate, paella, Padrón peppers, chorizo and churros). Order several plates to share and ask what is on special.

A special occasion

Aqui (Kammakargatan 9, 111 40 Stockholm, Sweden) — ★ 4.3 from 2,079 reviews; the menu confirms Spanish omelette (tortilla española), croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, garlic prawns (gambas al ajillo), fried calamari, pan con tomate, Padrón peppers, chorizo and churros. Tell them you are celebrating when you book; Spanish restaurants are usually generous with a celebration table.

A quick, casual bite

Bar Nombre (Odengatan 36, 113 51 Stockholm, Sweden) — ★ 4.6 from 1,139 reviews; the menu confirms Galician-style octopus (pulpo a la gallega) and oxtail stew (rabo de toro). Bar seating and a couple of tapas is the fastest way to eat well here.

Something sweet to finish

El Cielo - Tapas & Cava (Fleminggatan 22a, 112 32 Stockholm, Sweden) — one of the few places in Stockholm whose menu confirms churros.
