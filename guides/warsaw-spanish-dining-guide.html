Spanish food in Warsaw: a guide by occasion

Updated 10 Sep 2026 · Based on 11 tracked Spanish venues in Warsaw · Best-ranked list

Not every Spanish restaurant fits every plan. Using the Google ratings, review counts and the dishes we have confirmed on each menu, here is how we would play Warsaw depending on the occasion.

For a date

Pablo Tapas (Grzybowska 5a, 00-132 Warszawa, Poland) — the strongest all-round rating in the city (★ 4.5 from 2,797 reviews). Book ahead at weekends.

Tapas with friends

Sol y Sombra Tapas Bar (Grzybowska 2/16, 00-131 Warszawa, Poland) — the widest confirmed Spanish spread in Warsaw: Ibérico/serrano ham, paella, Padrón peppers and chorizo (★ 4.6 from 2,820 reviews; the menu confirms Ibérico/serrano ham, paella, Padrón peppers and chorizo). Order several plates to share and ask what is on special.

A special occasion

Tres Toros (Dobra 40, 00-344 Warszawa, Poland) — ★ 4.5 from 1,504 reviews; the menu confirms patatas bravas and Ibérico/serrano ham. Tell them you are celebrating when you book; Spanish restaurants are usually generous with a celebration table.

A quick, casual bite

Barceloneta (Kazimierzowska 43, 02-572 Warszawa, Poland) — ★ 4.5 from 313 reviews. Bar seating and a couple of tapas is the fastest way to eat well here.

Something sweet to finish

Sobremesa Tapas Bar Hala Koszyki (Hala Koszyki, Koszykowa 63, 00-667 Warszawa, Poland) — one of the few places in Warsaw whose menu confirms churros.
