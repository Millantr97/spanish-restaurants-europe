Spanish food in Tirana: a guide by occasion

Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Based on 5 tracked Spanish venues in Tirana · Best-ranked list

Not every Spanish restaurant fits every plan. Using the Google ratings, review counts and the dishes we have confirmed on each menu, here is how we would play Tirana depending on the occasion.

For a date

Tapas (Rr. Frosina Plaku, Kompleksi Magnet, Pallati Auriga Tirana AL, Tiranë 1003, Albania) — the strongest all-round rating in the city (★ 4.4 from 7 reviews; the menu confirms patatas bravas, croquetas, Ibérico/serrano ham, garlic prawns (gambas al ajillo), Galician-style octopus (pulpo a la gallega), fried calamari, paella, Padrón peppers, albóndigas (meatballs) and chorizo). Book ahead at weekends.

A special occasion

Oh, Lola! (Rruga Haxhi Kika, Tiranë 1022, Albania) — ★ 4.8 from 336 reviews. Tell them you are celebrating when you book; Spanish restaurants are usually generous with a celebration table.

A quick, casual bite

Allafrënga Tapas Bar (Rruga Qemal Stafa, Tiranë, Albania) — ★ 4.9 from 93 reviews. Bar seating and a couple of tapas is the fastest way to eat well here.
