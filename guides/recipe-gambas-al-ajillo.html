Gambas al ajillo (garlic prawns)

⏱ 15 min · Easy · 4 as a tapa · All recipes

Prawns sizzled in olive oil with garlic, dried chilli and a splash of sherry, served bubbling in a terracotta cazuela. The whole dish takes ten minutes; the trick is infusing the oil without burning the garlic.

Ingredients

  • 500 g raw prawns, peeled (tails on)
  • 6 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1-2 dried cayenne chillies (or a pinch of chilli flakes)
  • 150 ml olive oil
  • 2 tbsp fino sherry or white wine
  • Salt and chopped parsley
  • Crusty bread, mandatory

Method

  1. Heat the oil with the garlic and chilli over medium-low heat until the garlic just starts to turn golden at the edges.
  2. Add the prawns and salt, turn the heat up and cook 1-2 minutes per side until pink.
  3. Splash in the sherry, let it bubble for 30 seconds, scatter parsley and serve immediately in the cazuela, still sizzling.

Tips

  • The oil is the dish: mop it up with bread.
  • Do not overcook - prawns go rubbery in a minute. Off the heat they keep cooking, so serve fast.
