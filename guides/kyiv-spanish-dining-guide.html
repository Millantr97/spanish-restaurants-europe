Spanish food in Kyiv: a guide by occasion

Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Based on 5 tracked Spanish venues in Kyiv · Best-ranked list

Not every Spanish restaurant fits every plan. Using the Google ratings, review counts and the dishes we have confirmed on each menu, here is how we would play Kyiv depending on the occasion.

For a date

Buena Vista (Velyka Zhytomyrska St, 8/14, Kyiv, Ukraine, 01025) — the strongest all-round rating in the city (★ 4.3 from 2,425 reviews). Book ahead at weekends.

Tapas with friends

Dos Amigos (Saksahanskoho St, 51/87, Kyiv, Ukraine, 01033) — a solid all-round tapas option (★ 4.6 from 1,482 reviews). Order several plates to share and ask what is on special.

A special occasion

Boca a Boca (Mala Shyianovska St, 14/13, Kyiv, Ukraine, 01133) — ★ 4.6 from 317 reviews. Tell them you are celebrating when you book; Spanish restaurants are usually generous with a celebration table.

A quick, casual bite

Etno Tapas-Bar ТОВ (Prorizna St, 23А, Kyiv, Kyivs'ka oblast, Ukraine, 01034) — ★ 4.5 from 2 reviews. Bar seating and a couple of tapas is the fastest way to eat well here.
